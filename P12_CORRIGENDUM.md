# P12 evidence-interpretation corrigendum / P12 证据解释勘误

Status: **public `main` correction after v0.3.1; proposed for v0.3.2**. This page does not announce a new Release or change the frozen P12 result.

## 中文

`v0.3.1` 的“13项一致、P12一项部分一致”是已发布的**历史分类记录**。原先把原著 CESE 与另一研究 ASE 的起始尺寸差异作为 P12 的反对子项，依据不成立：两者不是同一估计量，不能仅凭不同起点断言同口径不一致。保留历史标签便于追溯，**不表示**本次重新证明了“部分一致”，也不自动改判为“一致”。

原著 N12/N14/N16/N18/N20/N22 六点公开表值的算术与账本内尺寸变化已核验；这不是六点独立量子化学复算。已有 N12/N14/N16 指定构型的本地候选计算仅提供有限方向相容性，尚未证明与原著 2011 方法、同基组逐态几何优化完全对齐；N18/N20/N22 没有相应独立 CESE 候选面板。**这些本地候选数值未纳入本公开包，不能作为公开可复核的新增结果引用。**

另一研究的 ASE/ISE-II 结果只为大环总体趋势提供不同估计量的旁证，不能复现或反证 CESE 的精确 N=16/18 起点。当前记录既未建立同估计量反例，也未独立确认该精确起点；没有反例的已审记录不等于证明所有未测尺寸都不存在反例。

通俗地说：**大环总体变化方向相容，精确尺寸边界尚未独立确认。** 不同文献可能因指标、分子状态、构型和判定标准不同而报告不同边界；这不是单纯的小数末位差异，也不是总体趋势已被推翻。要正式更改 P12 整项分类，须另立版本化决定并同步机器合同、矩阵和报告。

## English

The `v0.3.1` count of **13 consistent and one partially consistent (P12)** is a **historical published classification**. The previous use of different CESE and ASE onset sizes as an opposing P12 subclaim is not valid: these are different estimands. Retaining the label for traceability neither reaffirms its old rationale nor upgrades P12 to “consistent.”

The arithmetic and internal size trend of the monograph's six published N12/N14/N16/N18/N20/N22 values have been checked. This is not six-point independent quantum-chemical reproduction. Existing local specified-configuration N12/N14/N16 candidates offer limited directional compatibility, but do not establish full 2011-method, same-basis state-specific optimization identity; no corresponding independent N18/N20/N22 CESE candidate panel exists. **Those local candidate numbers are not packaged here as publicly reproducible new evidence.**

Independent ASE/ISE-II is a different-estimand complement for the broad large-ring trend, not a direct test of the precise CESE N=16/18 onset. The reviewed record establishes neither a same-estimand counterexample nor independent confirmation of that onset. Different studies can report different size boundaries under different descriptors, molecular states, geometries and criteria. The broad direction is compatible; the precise boundary remains open. This is a substantive question, not a last-decimal program difference.

The frozen `v0.3.1` [P12 machine result](evidence/P01-P14/P12/result.json), protocol and Release assets remain unchanged. Any future whole-proposition reclassification requires a separate versioned decision and synchronized machine contract, evidence reports, matrix and summaries.
