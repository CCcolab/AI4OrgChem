# AI4OrgChem P01–P14 evidence matrix

[Chinese matrix](P01-P14_evidence_matrix_zh-CN.md)

This is an English presentation of the published **v0.3.1** classification and its scope. The published snapshot records **13 consistent**, **1 partially consistent (P12)**, **0 globally inconsistent**, and **0 unknown**. The subsequent [P12 evidence-interpretation note](../P12_CORRIGENDUM.md#english) corrects an invalid reason for the historical partial label: CESE and ASE onset sizes are different estimands, so their difference is not a same-estimand counterexample. Keeping the published label does not reaffirm that reason or automatically upgrade P12. The six published values have an arithmetic and source-ledger check, not a six-point independent QM rerun; the precise N=16/18 onset remains independently unconfirmed.

“Consistent” means that evidence obtained under the registered protocol supports the corresponding monograph proposition within its tested scope. It does not mean fourteen equal-status independent QM reproductions, historical-code identity, or a universal law. This table separates familiar textbook narratives, the tested counter-proposition, evidence identity, and limitations. The [English evidence navigator](../evidence/P01-P14/README.md) links the frozen records; some underlying original records are in Chinese.

## Overall conclusion

Within frozen tested domains, the evidence supports the monograph's methodological challenge to treating conjugative stabilization, planarization, or steric destabilization as unconditional sufficient explanations. It challenges the **universality of certain heuristics**, not organic chemistry as a whole, and establishes no universal opposite law of conjugative destabilization.
