# AI4OrgChem Publication Positioning

## Proposed title

**Independent Computational Tests of Counter-Traditional Propositions in Organic Chemistry: Fourteen Reproducible LFMO and Energy-Based Assessments**

## Central conclusion

Without using the monograph's original program code, AI4OrgChem used AI-assisted engineering and layered computational and literature evidence to assess fourteen testable propositions. The published `v0.3.1` classification recorded thirteen consistent with the monograph and one (P12) partially consistent; none was globally inconsistent. The [P12 corrigendum](../P12_CORRIGENDUM.md#english) rejects the old cross-estimand opposing-subclaim rationale without assigning a new whole-proposition verdict. P05 now includes a seven-angle continuation in the same molecular system, with all six nonzero endpoints positive. P07 now combines an independent same-Hamiltonian five-state path audit with the earlier `DERIVED` proposition-level synthesis: an explicit bridge or path-complete sigma definition is required for energy closure. P11 changed only after a source-2007 state-specific all-planar recalculation reproduced both CE and IE, with the old fixed-geometry result retained for audit. Post-release review also exposed and corrected a P14 evidence-eligibility defect. The evidence materially challenges the unconditional generalization of several textbook heuristics, but does not establish that traditional organic chemistry is globally incorrect or that conjugation is universally destabilizing.

## Defensible claims

1. Fourteen proposition-level, reproducible and falsifiable evidence chains were produced.
2. Several tested systems contradict the use of conjugative stabilization/planarization and steric destabilization as universally sufficient explanations.
3. State-specific LFMO comparisons provide an operational analysis distinct from ordinary relaxed RHF total-energy differences.
4. The historical P12 label and its corrected evidence interpretation, the superseded P11 fixed-geometry result, and the P14 review-defect-and-repair trail remain public, demonstrating that the workflow was not designed as one-way advocacy.
5. AI assisted protocol execution, data organization, verification, generalization and explanation; the scientific evidence remains quantum-chemical and definition-dependent.

## Claims not supported

- “OpenAI or AI certified the monograph as correct”;
- “traditional organic chemistry has been overturned”;
- “all conjugation is destabilizing”;
- “the complete historical code and coordinate environment was reproduced”;
- “a learned model is itself an independent quantum-chemical proof.”
