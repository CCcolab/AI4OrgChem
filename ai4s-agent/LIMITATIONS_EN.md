# Limitations and scope

[Chinese original](LIMITATIONS_zh-CN.md)

- Some tasks use explicitly identified source-aligned or source-proxy inputs. They are not identity reproductions of the historical program or Cartesian coordinates.
- The ML dataset covers only 17 geometries, three families, and five targets. It does not support industrial-scale or arbitrary-molecule generalization.
- Post-return active-learning model effects are mixed.
- PySR outputs are exploratory candidate relations, not universally validated chemical laws.
- The interactive Agent navigates evidence and explains its scope; it is not an independent scientific adjudicator.
- The project challenges unconditional generalization of classical heuristics, without declaring all traditional organic chemistry wrong or establishing a universal opposite law.
